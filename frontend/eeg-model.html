Operational Workflow

01

Signal Acquisition

Electrical impulses from the cerebral cortex are captured in micro-volts using high-resolution biosensors across Alpha, Beta, Theta, and Delta bands.

02

Neural Processing

Raw EEG data is filtered and processed using FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) to extract specific frequency features for classification.

03

AI Inference

Our pre-trained Machine Learning model analyzes the patterns to provide objective scores for stress, focus, and overall mental state.
